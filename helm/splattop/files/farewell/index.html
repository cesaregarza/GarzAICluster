Thanks for everything

2023–2026

English

After more than three years, splat.top has closed.

This site will always be special to me, and it’s a little emotional to shut it down. It was my first real website, my first async app, and the first thing I ever ran on Kubernetes. It was also where I learned Helm and Argo CD. Most of what I know about platform engineering started here.

Financially, it never really made sense: no ads, not enough traffic to justify everything running behind it. It was expensive to run! But that was never the point. I loved being able to capture the data and show who the best Splatoon players were. That was magical.

It’s run its course now, and I need those resources for other things.

To everyone who used it over the years, thank you. Big thanks to hfcRed, SlushieGoose, niyari, and zxhie for their help with the project. I’m deeply grateful.

简体中文

运营三年多后，splat.top 已停止服务。

这个网站对我来说永远都很特别，要把它关掉，心里确实有些不舍。这是我第一个真正的网站、第一个异步应用，也是我第一次在 Kubernetes 上运行的项目。我也是在这里学会了 Helm 和 Argo CD。我对平台工程的大部分认识，都是从这里开始的。

从经济上说，它从来都不太划算：没有广告，访问量也不足以支撑背后运行的整套基础设施。维护它真的很花钱！但这从来不是我做它的目的。我喜欢收集这些数据，让大家看到谁是最出色的斯普拉遁玩家。那种感觉很奇妙。

如今，它已经完成了自己的使命，我也需要把这些资源用在其他事情上。

感谢这些年来每一位使用过它的人。特别感谢 hfcRed、SlushieGoose、niyari 和 zxhie 对这个项目的帮助。我由衷地感谢大家。
